2 Learning objective and scope

Read the Hebrew from a visual scene rather than receiving an English answer first. The desktop hero has the compact Hebrew verse on the physical LEFT and the scene on the physical RIGHT. Hebrew itself reads RTL. Retain niqqud, cantillation, punctuation, and correct combining-mark placement. Keep grammatical cues compact enough that the verse remains readable as a verse, not a wall of flashcards.

Initial real content is Proverbs 26:1 only. It has nine word tokens, five nouns, two adjectives and two particles. Six words are masculine, honor is both/common, and the particles have gender N/A. All inflected tokens here are singular. There are NO verbs, feminine words, plural/dual words or sourced exceptional/suppletive forms. Therefore no such marks may be fabricated merely to demonstrate the legend. Five nouns show centered i for absolute state; adjectives do not get noun-state superscripts. Synthetic forms belong only in explicitly labeled developer fixtures, never in this lesson.

Exact canonical verse:
כַּשֶּׁ֤לֶג ׀ בַּקַּ֗יִץ וְכַמָּטָ֥ר בַּקָּצִ֑יר כֵּ֤ן לֹא־נָאוֶ֖ה לִכְסִ֣יל כָּבֽוֹד׃

App-authored translation, hidden initially:
Like snow in summer and rain at harvest, so honor is not fitting for a fool.

Larger pericopes, additional books and Greek are a roadmap, not implemented or authorized content expansion. Do not translate the Hebrew-specific encoding automatically onto Greek.

Top: small project identity/reference, compact RTL verse and scene. The scene is explicitly an imaginative learning aid, not a historical depiction or morphology source. Do not show an English translation or English word-by-word glosses by default. Keep translation behind Show translation; it can open and close repeatedly without changing the lesson.

Below: seven-row grammar legend, then visible lower word/root cards, exact canonical verse reveal, reading key and sources. Do not move a wall of root labels or English glosses into the top line. Current stickers do not implement a root-browser or alternate-sense selector; do not claim click behavior that is not present.

Keep exactly seven separately labeled legend rows:
  Gender; Number; Noun state; Verb stem LEFT; Verb conjugation RIGHT;
  Icon outline POS; Form pattern.
Use VERB CONJUGATION as the primary category label. Use qatal / yiqtol as
primary labels; perfect / imperfect belong only in explanatory help as
traditional terminology. Stem remains a separate category. The last row must
explain both solid exceptional/suppletive and dotted predictable-weak cues.
These terminology/cue changes supersede the older Verb form / True irregular
labels still found in earlier snapshots.
Show ALL seven stem codes and ALL six form codes, not a few examples. Include a small note for affix dots, pending icons and local-code status. The current seven-row arrangement is approved; enhance it rather than replace it with an unrelated layout.

Latest requested addition: each legend item needs an explanatory hover help surface AND equivalent keyboard-focus and tap access. Hover alone or a title attribute alone is insufficient. Preserve compact closed appearance. Use semantic, focusable triggers; expose expanded state and the help association; support tap toggle, dismiss/close and Escape where appropriate, and never trap focus. Avoid accidental toggles when selecting Hebrew. Help must not obscure the verse permanently. Test repeat open/close and moving from one item to another. This addition is now implemented in the Python prototype with 29 legend controls. Hover or focus shows help; click/tap pins or unpins it; Escape or blur closes it; right-edge viewport clamping is included. Each trigger is a button with aria-describedby and aria-expanded, paired with a hidden role=tooltip element. Flagged words use a focusable role=button trigger with Enter/Space activation and the same source-note help. A native title fallback remains. These interactions passed a minimal-DOM test; actual browser rendering and screen-reader behavior still require target-computer QA.

Help should clarify grammar rather than promise fixed translations. Verb stem summaries must be nuanced: Qal is the basic stem, not always an active English verb; Niphal often passive/middle; Piel has varied lexical functions, not always intensive; Pual is often the passive counterpart of Piel; Hiphil often causative; Hophal often passive causative; Hithpael often reflexive/middle, sometimes reciprocal. Existing wording is stored in legend_help_chirho.json; its publisher-source map is legend_sources_chirho.json, using unfoldingWord Hebrew Grammar at https://uhg.readthedocs.io/en/stable/. The prototype worker verified those primary grammar sources on 2026-10-26. Preserve those citations and review any wording changes; the grammar sources do not establish the custom numeric/letter code. Qatal/yiqtol (traditionally called perfect/imperfect) are Hebrew conjugation labels, not a universal past/future switch. Keep those traditional English labels secondary in help, not primary legend labels. The broad app category also includes participles and infinitives; explain that pedagogical grouping without claiming all members are finite conjugations. Distinguish sourced wayyiqtol from simple conjunction plus yiqtol in future data; preserve jussive/cohortative distinctions when provided, without inventing additional present codes. Do not derive these from the presence of waw alone.

Responsive baseline: desktop two columns; at <=640 px current prototype stacks verse before scene. Existing verse font is about 43 px desktop / 36 px mobile, generous mark clearance; font stack SBL Hebrew, Ezra SIL, Noto Serif Hebrew, Times New Roman, serif. No external font download currently occurs. Ensure all glyphs render; if bundling a font later, verify its license. Wrap between tokens, never inside a base+marks cluster. Test narrow 320/390 px and desktop 1440 px. No horizontal page/card overflow. Accessible text must communicate color-coded categories; color alone is not enough. English text uses LTR isolation without corrupting Hebrew reading order.

5 Unicode and source integrity

Canonical verse is an immutable source string, independent of decorated rendering. Preserve original code points, niqqud, cantillation, paseq, maqaf, sof pasuq and spaces. Do NOT normalize the stored canonical source to NFC. OSHB documentation warns about normalization. Internal normalized comparisons may validate equivalence but must not replace source bytes.

Source slash-separated affix boundaries become DISPLAY-ONLY dots. Never insert dots inside a base letter plus following combining marks. Every segment must be nonempty, must not begin with a combining mark, and must reconstruct its token. The current code compares reconstruction under NFD while retaining source strings. Future import validation should also record exact reconstruction and flag any reordered source data rather than silently repair it. Keep canonical copy/export free of teaching dots, icon placeholders and superscripts.

The current renderer locates token strings sequentially in canonical text and carries intervening punctuation unchanged. For a growing corpus, use stable token IDs plus explicit source spans or ordered word/punctuation records so repeated words are not ambiguous. Maintain a regression that stripping only annotation DOM from compact rendering reproduces the exact canonical verse. Never strip Hebrew marks to simplify rendering.

6 Current data contract

lesson_chirho.json top level:
  reference: string
  verse: exact Unicode canonical string
  translation: app-authored English learning gloss
  note: explanatory caveats
  sources: array of {title, url}
  tokens: ordered array of token objects
Current token fields:
  text: canonical token string
  segments: string[] sourced display boundaries
  pos: noun | verb | adjective | particle | unknown
  gender: masculine | feminine | common | na | unknown
  number: singular | plural | dual | na | unknown
  state: absolute | construct | na | unknown (nullable future)
  root: Hebrew string or null; NOT lemma
  gloss, status, analysis, source_note: strings
  root_icon: optional local .png path (currently app folder or assets/ only)
  icon_alt: optional contextual mnemonic description
  verb_code: optional exactly one digit 1-7 plus P/I/M/A/C/B, verbs only
  form_note: optional {kind, text, source}
    kind = predictable_weak | exceptional | suppletive
    text must explain the actual form; source must be an HTTP(S) URL
Current missing/invalid form_note is ignored for marking. Strengthen schema validation to report malformed curated data rather than silently hide errors in future authoring tooling.

sample-source-chirho.json is separate evidence, not another editable rendering model. It records OSIS reference, source URL/hash, licensing, attribution and word/punctuation records. Word records include OSHB stable id, source_segmented, raw morph, lemma_id, lexical_form, per-segment morphology/role, root_entry and root_note. Preserve this provenance when migrating; do not throw it away after deriving UI fields.

Recommended versioned future model, NOT yet implemented:

Use explicit unknown / N/A / unresolved states. A null root can mean unresolved or not applicable; add a reason field rather than treating both as the same fact. Use separate curated field-level evidence where available. Do not require a database for one lesson.

Root is not lemma. One root can have multiple meanings; the selected icon is contextual. Reuse a snow/rain icon only in a verified matching sense, not merely on a matching consonant string. Future root_icon_alternatives may list sense, filename and source explicitly; selection UI and automatic alternatives do not exist now. Do not equate homographs or invent a single etymology by stripping affixes.
